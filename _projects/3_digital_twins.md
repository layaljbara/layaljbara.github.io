I am developing **digital twins** for solid organ transplant patients - multi-domain models that support **counterfactual treatment simulation** (with vs without therapy) under uncertainty.

The framework combines domain modules (metabolic, liver, cardiovascular, renal, and related systems), hybrid mechanistic and statistical / ML models, and external validation - toward individualized treatment-effect estimates in transplant populations.

### Code & data availability

- **Status:** Active research codebase (private).
- **Code:** Available **upon request** for collaboration, subject to institutional agreements.
- **Data:** Patient-level transplant trajectories are **not** publicly shareable.
- **Public overview:** [transplant-ai-projects](https://github.com/layaljbara/transplant-ai-projects) (structure and description only).
