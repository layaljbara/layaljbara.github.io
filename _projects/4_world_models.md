Beyond single-visit prediction, I am developing **world-model and time-series** approaches for liver transplant care - models that learn how a patient’s state evolves across clinic visits, labs, and notes.

The aim is a longitudinal representation that can support next-step clinical reasoning (labs, immunosuppression, and related decisions) without collapsing the patient into a static snapshot. This track sits alongside the [clinical language models]({{ '/projects/1_clinical_llms/' | relative_url }}) and [digital pathology]({{ '/projects/2_pathology/' | relative_url }}) work.

Methods and unpublished numbers stay off this page until they are in a preprint.

### Code & data availability

- **Status:** Active research codebase (private).
- **Code:** Available **upon request** for collaboration, subject to institutional agreements.
- **Data:** Longitudinal clinical signals are **not** publicly shareable.
