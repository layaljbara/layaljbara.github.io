I am an **AI postdoctoral researcher** at [University Health Network](https://www.uhn.ca/) in the Multiorgan Transplant Program. I work in the [Bhat Lab](https://bhatlab.ca/) (*Precision Medicine to Enhance Life after Liver Transplantation*) and [IMPACT-AI](https://divya-sharma-york.github.io) (*Innovative Machine Learning for Precision, Analytics, Care & Treatment*), led by Dr. Divya Sharma at York University.

My research builds **clinical AI for liver transplantation** across four tracks: **clinical language models** on post-transplant clinic notes; **digital pathology and image models** for biopsy-level follow-up; **world models / time-series** for longitudinal trajectories; and **digital twins** for counterfactual treatment simulation in solid organ transplant.

Before UHN I completed a PhD in Chemical and Biological Engineering at the [University of British Columbia](https://www.ubc.ca/), where I was a [Vanier Scholar](https://vanier.gc.ca/) working on improving CFD for biological flows with machine learning, including graph neural networks and physics-inspired models.

Outside of research I like staying outdoors and active: **running** and **kayaking**.
